Privacy Policy

Elite Industrial Services

Effective Date: September 7, 2026

Elite Industrial Services ("EIS," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through our website, eisdiv.com (the "Site"), how we use it, and the choices you have. By using the Site, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

Information You Provide to Us

When you submit our contact form, we collect the information you enter, which may include:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Company name
  • Service type requested (Maintenance, Installation, Repair, or Consultation)
  • Project details you provide in the message field

We use this information only to respond to your inquiry and discuss the services you've requested. We do not currently offer online purchases, user accounts, or any other means of submitting personal information through the Site.

Information Collected Automatically

We may use website analytics tools, such as Google Analytics, and similar cookie- or pixel-based technologies to understand how visitors use our Site — for example, which pages are viewed, how long visitors stay, and what device or browser they use. This information is generally collected in aggregate and is not used to personally identify you. Where we implement analytics, the provider's own privacy policy will govern how they process this data (for Google Analytics, see policies.google.com/privacy).

Our web server may also automatically log standard technical information, such as your IP address, browser type, and the date and time of your visit, for security and site-performance purposes

2. Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device. If and when analytics tools are added to this Site, they may use cookies to collect the usage information described above. You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings; note that some Site features may not function properly if cookies are disabled.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to inquiries submitted through our contact form
  • Provide quotes, schedule consultations, and deliver the services you request
  • Maintain and improve the Site and our services
  • Comply with legal obligations

    • We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for their own marketing purposes.

4. How We Share Information

We may share information with:

  • Service providers who help us operate the Site or our business (such as our web host, email provider, and, if implemented, analytics providers), who are only permitted to use it to provide services to us

  • Business transfers, if EIS is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, in which case information may be transferred as part of that transaction

5. Data Retention

We retain information submitted through our contact form for as long as needed to respond to your inquiry and for a reasonable period afterward for our business records, unless you ask us to delete it sooner (see Section 7).

6. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect the information we collect. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Choices
