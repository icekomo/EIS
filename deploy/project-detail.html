The Challenge

The Mid-Continent Refinery Upgrade was a flagship project for Elite Industrial Services, involving the modernization of the plant's main distillation unit and the installation of over 5,000 linear feet of high-pressure process piping. As a critical facility for regional fuel production, minimizing downtime was a primary objective.

Our Solution

Elite Industrial Services implemented an off-site modular fabrication strategy. By fabricating major piping spools and equipment skids in our controlled facility, we reduced on-site labor requirements by 40% and significantly mitigated safety risks.

  • Advanced 3D laser scanning for precise field measurements.
  • Implementation of a dedicated on-site safety coordinator.
  • Night-shift mobilization for critical heavy lifts during low-traffic periods.

The Result

The refinery upgrade resulted in a 12% increase in throughput capacity for the facility and a significant reduction in emissions. The client reported that the integration of the new systems was seamless, with the commissioning phase completed in record time.
